PsychInterview Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Patient records stay on your device

Everything you type into PsychInterview, including patient names, interviews, mental status examinations and notes, is saved only inside the app on your own iPhone, iPad or Mac. The developer does not run a server for the app, and the app does not send your records to the developer or to anyone else. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Files you choose to share

When you export a Word, PowerPoint, PDF or backup file, the app hands it to your device's share sheet. Where it goes next (for example Files, Mail or your hospital's storage) is your choice, and that service's own privacy policy applies. Store patient files only where your hospital allows.

Subscription

Purchases are handled by Apple. The app checks with Apple whether your subscription is active; the developer receives only the anonymous sales reports Apple gives every developer, never your name or payment details. Manage or cancel the subscription in Settings > your name > Subscriptions.

Deleting your data

Delete a record in the app, or delete the app to remove all of its records from the device. Backup files you saved elsewhere are not affected.

Children

PsychInterview is a tool for healthcare professionals and trainees, and is not meant for children.

Medical disclaimer

PsychInterview is a documentation and training aid. It does not diagnose or give medical advice; clinical decisions remain the clinician's responsibility.
